# ISO/IEC 42001:2023: AI Management Systems

**Source:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023 *Information technology - Artificial intelligence - Management system*, published by ISO and IEC in December 2023. Authoritative text: the ISO publication (paid access via [iso.org](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)).

**Scope:** ISO/IEC 42001 is a voluntary management-system standard for organisations that develop, deploy, or use AI systems. It is structured like other ISO management-system standards (9001, 27001), with a high-level structure covering planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.

This document maps the clauses with explicit record-keeping or operational-control requirements to the Provenex receipt field or operational artifact that produces evidence for them. Clauses dealing with policy authorship, leadership commitment, and competence are organisational and out of scope for a technical mapping.

## Clause 7.5: documented information

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 clause 7.5 covers documented information that the AI management system requires. Subclauses 7.5.1 (general), 7.5.2 (creating and updating), and 7.5.3 (control of documented information) describe what must be captured and how it must be controlled.

| Subclause requirement | Provenex artifact |
| --- | --- |
| 7.5.1 documented information required by the standard | Receipts are the documented record of agent actions. Schema is fixed (`schema_version`); fields are listed in [`docs/receipt_format.md`](../receipt_format.md). |
| 7.5.2 identification and description (title, date, author, reference numbers) | `receipt_id` (unique identifier), `issued_at` (date), `caller_hash` and `policy.*.policy_id` (author/authority reference), `schema_version` (format reference). |
| 7.5.2 format and media | JSON, UTF-8, canonicalised. Same canonicalisation rule that governs the signature (`sort_keys=True`, no whitespace, `ensure_ascii=False`). |
| 7.5.2 review and approval | `signature` block. The producer's signature is the approval mark. Re-verification is the review step. |
| 7.5.3 access and retrieval | Receipts addressable by `receipt_id`, `trajectory_id`, `caller_hash`. SIEM-side indexing via OCSF mapping. |
| 7.5.3 storage and preservation (legibility) | Receipt schema is stable across versions (additive only at minor versions; see [`docs/receipt_format.md` § Versioning](../receipt_format.md#versioning)). Old receipts remain machine-readable. |
| 7.5.3 control of changes | Receipts are immutable post-signature. Any change invalidates the signature and is detectable by anyone holding the public key. The hash-chained `WitnessLog` makes silent drop or reorder detectable. |
| 7.5.3 retention and disposition | [`docs/receipt_retention.md`](../receipt_retention.md) Terraform module: S3 Object Lock + Glacier, 7-year default. |

## Clause 8.1: operational planning and control

> The organization shall plan, implement and control the processes... including:
> (a) establishing criteria for the processes;
> (b) implementing control of the processes in accordance with the criteria.

| Provenex artifact | What it provides for clause 8.1 |
| --- | --- |
| Unified YAML policy (`verification` + `access_control` + `tool_call_control` halves) | Process criteria expressed as machine-readable rules. The policy text is the criteria document. |
| `policy.*.policy_id` plus `policy.*.policy_version_hash` on every receipt | Evidence that the in-effect criteria at run time were the recorded ones. Editing the policy produces a new hash; the change point is detectable across the receipt archive. |
| `provenex policy validate <file.yaml>` | CI-time check that the policy file parses and is well-formed. |
| `provenex policy hash <file.yaml>` | Prints the canonical `policy_version_hash` so CI can pin and compare. |
| `provenex policy simulate` (commercial; see [`docs/policy.md`](../policy.md)) | Replay historical receipts against a candidate new policy; surfaces what would have changed. |

## Clause 8.2: AI risk assessment, 8.3: AI risk treatment

Clauses 8.2 and 8.3 are governance processes. Provenex contributes the technical evidence stream into them:

| Provenex artifact | Risk-treatment role |
| --- | --- |
| Five verification outcomes plus policy deny decisions | Per-event risk classification. `TAMPERED` is a higher-severity event than `STALE`; `UNAUTHORIZED` denies on tool calls trigger downstream review. |
| `caller_hash` aggregation over a window | Per-actor risk picture. Suitable for input to a UEBA layer. |
| SIEM streaming with OCSF mapping | Routes per-event evidence into the organisation's existing risk treatment workflow. |

## Clause 9.1: monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation

> The organization shall determine:
> (a) what needs to be monitored and measured;
> (b) the methods for monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation;
> ...
> (d) when the results from monitoring and measurement shall be analysed and evaluated.

| Provenex artifact | Monitoring role |
| --- | --- |
| Receipts as the per-event measurement | Each receipt is one measured event with structured fields. |
| Prometheus `/metrics` endpoint on `provenex-server` ([`docs/streaming_export_ops.md`](../streaming_export_ops.md)) | Aggregate counters: receipts per second, deny rate, verification-outcome distribution. |
| `provenex index audit --threshold-days <n>` | Periodic monitoring of index health. Catches re-ingest paths that skipped Provenex. |
| `provenex selftest` | Conformance check against the installed binary. Suitable as a scheduled job. |

## Clause 9.2: internal audit, 9.3: management review

Internal audit and management review are organisational processes. The Provenex receipt archive is one of the inputs:

| Provenex artifact | Audit role |
| --- | --- |
| `provenex audit --trajectory <dir>` | Replays a stored trajectory; one PASS/FAIL across many receipts. |
| The ~25-line pure-stdlib reference verifier in [`docs/regulator_verification.md`](../regulator_verification.md) | Confirms the audit pipeline matches the documented algorithm; useful in internal-audit independence reviews. |
| `WitnessLog` checkpoint signatures from external witnesses | Independent attestation that the log is not a fork. Strengthens audit independence. |

## What this mapping does not cover

- Clauses on **leadership** (5.x), **planning** (6.x), and **competence** (7.2) are organisational and not directly addressed by any technical artifact.
- Clause **10 (improvement)** is a process clause. Receipts feed into improvement reviews; they do not constitute the review itself.
- The **Annex A controls** in ISO/IEC 42001:2023 are specific control objectives. A control-by-control mapping is out of scope for this document; the receipt fields above are the underlying evidence base from which control-level mappings are derived.

For the verification path, see [`docs/regulator_verification.md`](../regulator_verification.md). For policy authoring, see [`docs/policy.md`](../policy.md).
